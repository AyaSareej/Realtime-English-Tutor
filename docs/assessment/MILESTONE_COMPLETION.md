Software implementation is complete for the 0.7.0 MVP. The 79-test suite verifies the software contract; it does not establish CEFR or fluency cut-score validity. Three evidence-producing activities cannot be truthfully manufactured in code: human assessment agreement, human fluency calibration, and the teammate phoneme model's real output. Their interfaces, templates, acceptance calculations, and failure isolation are complete and ready to run. Guided performance is fixed-scenario oral-reading practice and cannot alter the controlled placement result.
